📖 How To Play Geometry Quest 3D

🎮 Controls & Exploration

  • W A S D - Move player in 3D
  • SHIFT - Sprint / Run faster
  • SPACE - Jump over obstacles & fountains
  • MOUSE - Look around and orbit camera
  • E - Interact with doors, NPCs, & challenge terminals
  • ESC - Open Pause Menu

  • Explore 10 Themed Rooms: Each dedicated to core geometry topics.
  • Answer Questions: Beat the 30-second timer and maintain combos.
  • Earn 3 Stars: High accuracy and fast answers award 3 stars per topic.
  • Level Up: Gain XP and unlock the Final Master Arena!
  • Collect Tokens: Grab glowing geometric shapes in the courtyard.
